About

I’m an experienced Full Stack Developer and Cybersecurity Specialist with over 3 years of proven expertise in building fast, scalable, and interactive web applications using React, Next.js, Vanilla JavaScript, and TypeScript. I specialize in server-side rendering, static site generation, and cross-platform mobile development with Flutter and React Native. Backed by a degree in Cybersecurity from Bayero University, Kano, I bring a security-first mindset to full stack development—ensuring every interface and backend architecture I build is not only visually compelling but also secure, performant, and optimized for accessibility and SEO. Detail-oriented and analytical, I have a strong foundation in threat detection, incident response, security monitoring, and secure system architecture. I am adept at applying industry frameworks including MITRE ATT&CK, NIST Incident Response Lifecycle, and OWASP Top 10 to identify, analyze, and mitigate security risks. Over the years, I’ve mentored junior developers, led training sessions on web, mobile, and cyber technologies, and contributed to both freelance and open-source projects. I thrive in collaborative, fast-paced environments and enjoy solving complex challenges through clean, efficient code and user-centered design. When I’m not coding, I explore emerging technologies, engage with developer communities, and create tools and content to help others grow in the tech space.
