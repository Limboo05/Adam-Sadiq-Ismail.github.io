About

I’m an experienced Full Stack Engineer & Cyber Security Analyst with over 3 years of proven expertise in building fast, scalable, and secure web/mobile applications using React, Next.js, TypeScript, Flutter, and Laravel. I specialize in server-side rendering, cross-platform mobile development, and most importantly — secure architecture with a defense-in-depth mindset.

Backed by a degree in Cybersecurity from Bayero University, Kano, and advanced SOC training (CompTIA Security+, CySA+, BTL1), I’ve actively worked as a **Security Analyst for ReadyUp Platform (UK)** where I maintain gateway security, monitor threat intelligence, and harden infrastructure. My security-first approach ensures every interface and backend I build is resilient against OWASP Top 10 threats, secured by robust authentication, and optimized for performance.

Additionally, I’ve mentored junior developers, led cybersecurity training with NYSC, and contributed to freelance & open-source security tools. When I'm not coding or defending networks, I explore emerging cyber threats, engage with developer communities, and design graphics that blend creativity with tech.
